# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Truck owners in the US and Canada who work on their own rigs. They arrive knowing their truck (year/make/model) and a symptom or part name, often from a phone in a garage or driveway. The job to be done: find the part that actually fits their specific truck and buy it without second-guessing the store's legitimacy.

## Product Purpose

Hot Rod Rigs is a single-store e-commerce storefront for truck parts and accessories. Core value: a truck owner can find the right part for their specific truck (by category or by truck make/model via the persistent "My Truck" fitment context) and buy it online with a smooth, trustworthy checkout. Success looks like: fitment confidence (no wrong-part returns), completed checkouts, and repeat buyers who keep their truck saved.

## Brand Personality

Tough and dependable. A serious garage, not a showroom: black, metallic, straight to the point. Confident and plainspoken; the interface talks like a good mechanic — tells you what fits, what it costs, and when it ships. No hype, no fake urgency.

## Anti-references

- Generic marketplace clutter (Amazon/AliExpress): badge soup, slashed prices, countdown timers, fake scarcity.
- The MarketPro template demo it was built from: generic tech-store sections, fabricated deals/reviews, multi-vendor noise.
- Faceless corporate auto-parts catalogs: flat B2B grids with zero personality.

## Design Principles

1. **Fitment first** — the customer's truck is the organizing context; surfaces should surface and respect it everywhere.
2. **Honest availability** — never fake stock, urgency, or social proof; show real lead times and true claims only.
3. **Garage-grade clarity** — high contrast, legible at arm's length on a phone in a dim garage; one obvious next action per surface.
4. **Tough, not loud** — dark, metallic restraint; the orange accent (#FA6400) is a torch, used sparingly for action and state.
5. **Earn trust visibly** — real policies, real contact, real shipping rates; every claim verifiable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA: ≥4.5:1 body-text contrast, visible focus states, keyboard-operable menus and dialogs, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all animation. Touch targets ≥44px on mobile commerce surfaces.
